# Mesting Music Beta.12 categorized search and local Back

Mode: Operate. Job: find a genuine song, album, artist, public playlist or lyric match, act on its real identity, and return without losing the current task. Authority is the confirmed Beta.12 return-continuity plan together with the inherited Beta.11 category reference and Mesting/Apple Music world. Reference blue is not a new global accent. PlatformCategorySearchRepository maps songs to `/search`, albums to `/search/album`, artists to `/search/artist`, playlists to `/search/special`, and lyric content to `/search?type=lyric`. `/search/lyric` is a download-candidate endpoint. Artist bare-list envelopes and playlist `gid` global identities are supported. Missing visible portraits use coalesced `/artist/detail` reads, at most three concurrent and 160 successful cached identities; failed fetches do not become permanent artwork. Album, artist and playlist details call `/album/songs`, `/artist/audios` and `/playlist/track/all` respectively; public playlist details require `global_collection_id`. Track actions reuse the single audio owner and account-guarded personal actions.

The song provider retains its existing paging; CategorySearchController holds separate on-demand slices for the other categories, each with items, page, loading, total, more and error. Query/account generations cancel or ignore stale work. Switching category preserves loaded content and its own scroll, without eagerly fetching all categories. Error/retry keeps existing rows; normal pagination remains automatic. Enter/exit pairing uses local motion and separately owned pane scroll controllers, including rapid A→B→A changes.

Search has two exit intentions. Bottom destination navigation ends the session: clear text/query/category/results, invalidate late responses and dispose the result-local PageStorageBucket when the landing replaces results; later searching the same text begins at offset zero and Songs. Search history is retained. Temporary player/album/artist/playlist routes suspend the same mounted search; returning restores query/category/results/offset. Beta.12 BetaSearchBackHistory adds real nested LocalHistoryEntry targets to the enclosing ModalRoute: results Back clears the query to landing, the next Back closes search to its actual origin. Post-frame synchronization, PopScope, BackButtonListener and both 32dp edge strips share **results → landing → originating page** across all five categories. It is Navigator history, not only a callback attached to a root page. Actual Android system-edge/predictive Back remains a device validation item. The isolated result bucket must not be promoted to the persistent route because it would resurrect offsets across ended sessions.
