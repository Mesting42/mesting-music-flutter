# Mesting Music Beta.11 categorized search

Mode: Operate. Job: find a genuine song, album, artist, public playlist or lyric match, act on its real identity, and return without losing the current task. Authority is the confirmed Beta.11 plan and the user's five-category reference within the inherited Mesting/Apple Music world. Reference blue is not a new global accent. No separate AI comp, QUALITY BAR card or random FORM seed was produced.

The first result viewport begins with the compact fixed-order **歌曲 / 专辑 / 歌手 / 歌单 / 歌词** rail and real results; it has no large “搜索结果” heading. Selected text uses the established Beta red. Songs keep the lazy 52dp-cover/identity/heart/More list and “共 N 首”. Album/playlist phone grids have two square-cover columns; artists use circular portraits and names; lyric rows use track identity and only snippets actually returned. No placeholder lyric or count is invented. Bottom search retains its real premium glass and safe-area/keyboard ownership.

PlatformCategorySearchRepository maps songs to `/search`, albums to `/search/album`, artists to `/search/artist`, playlists to `/search/special`, and lyric content to `/search?type=lyric`. `/search/lyric` is a download-candidate endpoint. Artist bare-list envelopes and playlist `gid` global identities are supported. Missing visible portraits use coalesced `/artist/detail` reads, at most three concurrent and 160 successful cached identities; failed fetches do not become permanent artwork. Album, artist and playlist details call `/album/songs`, `/artist/audios` and `/playlist/track/all` respectively; public playlist details require `global_collection_id`. Track actions reuse the single audio owner and account-guarded personal actions.

The song provider retains its existing paging; CategorySearchController holds separate on-demand slices for the other categories, each with items, page, loading, total, more and error. Query/account generations cancel or ignore stale work. Switching category preserves loaded content and its own scroll, without eagerly fetching all categories. Error/retry keeps existing rows; normal pagination remains automatic. Enter/exit pairing uses local motion and separately owned pane scroll controllers, including rapid A→B→A changes.

Search has two exit intentions. Bottom destination navigation ends the session: clear text/query/category/results, invalidate late responses and dispose the result-local PageStorageBucket when the landing replaces results; later searching the same text begins at offset zero and Songs. Search history is retained. Temporary player/album/artist/playlist routes suspend the same mounted search; returning restores query/category/results/offset. System/edge Back retains **results → landing → originating page**. An isolated bucket must not be promoted to the persistent route because it would resurrect offsets across ended sessions.
