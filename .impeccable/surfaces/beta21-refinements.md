  public transferable routes and keep selection native-confirmed. OS-owned
  pairing/authorization and unsupported transfers retain a system-management
  action, explicitly accepted by the user.
- Legal copy follows the actual Beta services and storage. Do not invent a
  contact, account deletion, successful listening-time credit or member reward.
  The user explicitly deferred contact information; this is not a legal audit.
